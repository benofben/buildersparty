# The Builders Party
A fantasy --- in mid 2020, a young governor dropped party affiliation, taking on the affiliation of a new party, The Builders Party.  Bloomberg backed the candidate who went on to win.  The Republican party disintegrated and the Democrats then coalesced around a liberal platform analogous to Nordic socialism.  They proceeded to lose most elections for the next 20 years while the Builders changed the landscape of the US both literally and figuratively.

## Overview
Fundamentally people create.  We are at our best when we are building and improving the environments around ourselves.  The USA was founded with the idea of enabling yeoman farmers to own farms, determining their destiny and leading productive, useful lives.  Since then, we’ve grown from an agrarian society to one where a single farmer can feed 300 people.  Those incredibly productive farmers, the backbone of our society, have enabled the majority of us to engage in ever more complex and specialized endeavours.

## Mascot
The mascot of the Builders Party is a busy beaver with a welding kit standing in front of the Stars and Stripes.  The Builder Party is plural, not possessive as we are a party made up of builders rather than a party belonging to builders.

## Infrastructure
A core tenant of the Builders Party is that building is what people like to do.  Government exists to help them build, bettering their own lives and the lives of those around them.
