# Background

I'm pretty disgusted with both the Democrats and Republicans.  They should present a useful dichotomoy:

* Democrats - High taxes funding large government social programs.
* Republicans - Low taxes with small government funding basic necessities such as roads and schools.

Unfortunately the two parties are currently both engaged in pandering populism.  There is no meaningful attempt to balance budgets or govern effectively.

I'm giving up on saying I'm a Democrat.  The Builder's Party is my attempt to reason through what I would like to see.
